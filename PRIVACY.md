# 隐私协议 / Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026年11月8日

1. 适用范围
本协议说明「色温检测」当前版本如何处理数据。制作、设计、工程、测试署名均为 PATRICK STAR。应用不要求注册账号，隐私协议和使用条款可在设置 → 关于中离线阅读。

2. 传感器与临时数据
应用在前台运行且未暂停时，通过系统传感器接口读取色温、后置照度及接口信息，用于实时显示和诊断。接口信息包括名称、厂商、类型、通道、订阅状态和原始事件。实时采样及趋势保存在当前会话内存中；退出前台或暂停时停止订阅，不在后台持续采样。厂商是否开放接口由设备系统决定。

3. 保存在本机的数据
只有点击保存后，应用才将该次测量、原始读数、采样时间、传感器来源和你填写的备注保存为记录。最多保留 100 条，超过时移除最早的记录。语言选择和传感器映射设置也保存在应用本地存储中。停止测量或切换语言不会删除已保存记录。

4. 权限与网络
当前版本没有联网权限，不提供账号、云同步、广告、行为统计或自动诊断上传，也不读取相机、麦克风、位置、联系人、短信和其他应用的文件。后置光线与色温通过系统开放的传感器接口读取，不需要相机权限。设备系统自身的数据处理不由本应用控制。

5. 剪贴板与主动导出
点击复制读数、复制记录或复制诊断时，相关文本会写入系统剪贴板；应用不主动读取剪贴板。诊断文本可能包含设备厂商、型号、系统版本、构建信息、传感器标识与原始事件。点击导出后，应用调用系统分享面板，仅向你选定的接收应用提供记录文本；后续处理遵循接收应用的规则。没有点击这些操作时，应用不会自行发送上述文本。

6. 删除与保留
你可以在记录详情中删除单条记录。通过系统清除本应用存储空间或卸载应用，可删除应用本地保存的记录与设置。应用不主动备份到云端。已经复制、分享或另行保存的副本，应在对应应用或存储位置自行删除。开发者没有远程恢复这些本地记录的服务。

7. 协议更新
后续版本若改变数据处理方式，会在应用内更新本协议及生效日期。本协议描述当前版本的实际功能，不作永不变化或绝对安全的保证。

1. Scope
This policy explains how the current version of Color Temperature handles data. Production, design, engineering and testing are credited to PATRICK STAR. No account is required. This policy and the Terms of Use are available offline under Settings > About.

2. Sensors and temporary data
While the app is in the foreground and not paused, it reads CCT, rear illuminance and interface information through system sensor APIs for live display and diagnostics. Interface information includes sensor names, vendors, types, channels, subscription states and raw events. Live samples and trends remain in session memory. Subscriptions stop when the app leaves the foreground or is paused; there is no continuous background sampling. Interface availability depends on the device system.

3. Data stored on this device
A measurement becomes a saved record only when you tap Save. Records include the measurement, raw values, sample times, sensor sources and your note. Up to 100 records are retained; the oldest records are removed when this limit is exceeded. Your language choice and sensor mapping settings are also stored locally. Stopping measurement or changing the language does not delete saved records.

4. Permissions and network
The current version has no internet permission, accounts, cloud sync, advertising, behavioral analytics or automatic diagnostic uploads. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, messages or files belonging to other apps. CCT and rear light are read through available system sensor interfaces without camera permission. Data processing performed by the device system is outside the control of this app.

5. Clipboard and sharing
Copying a reading, history or diagnostics writes the requested text to the system clipboard. The app does not proactively read the clipboard. Diagnostics may include the device manufacturer, model, system version, build information, sensor identifiers and raw events. Export opens the system share sheet and supplies record text only to the receiving app you choose. Further processing follows the rules of that app. These texts are not sent automatically without your action.

6. Deletion and retention
You can delete individual records from their details. Clearing app storage in system settings or uninstalling the app removes its locally stored records and preferences. The app does not initiate cloud backups. Copies you have shared, copied or saved elsewhere must be deleted in the corresponding app or location. The developer does not operate a remote recovery service for these local records.

7. Updates
If a future version changes data handling, this policy and its effective date will be updated in the app. This policy describes the current functionality and does not promise that it will never change or that data is absolutely secure.
